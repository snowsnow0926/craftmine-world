## 1 Copyright stays with the contributor

- The contributor keeps copyright in their contribution.
- This agreement is a licence grant, not an assignment, unless a separate written
  assignment is signed.

## 2 Grant to the project

The contributor grants the project a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free,
perpetual, irrevocable licence to:

- reproduce, modify, distribute and publicly perform the contribution;
- sublicense the contribution under the project's community licence
  (AGPL-3.0-only for the creation core, MIT for export runtime/example code, or the
  licence the file already carries);
- **relicense the contribution under other licence terms, including a proprietary
  commercial licence**, so the project can offer the commercial option described in
  `docs/LICENSING_STRATEGY.md` section 5.
